# Copilot Cowork Instructions: Citizen Enquiry Support Demo Assets

## Purpose

Use these instructions to create the fully synthetic demonstration environment required for the **Public Sector: Citizen Enquiry Support with Microsoft 365 Copilot** scenario.

The scenario follows a **Frontline Service Officer** responding to citizen enquiries during a developing essential-service disruption.

The reference implementation uses a fictional water supply disruption. The generated assets support a connected classroom demonstration across:

- Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat
- Outlook
- Excel
- Word
- Teams
- PowerPoint

Copilot Cowork is used only to prepare the synthetic demonstration environment before the class.

> **Copilot Cowork is not part of the learner-facing classroom demonstration.**

---

# Before You Begin

Confirm that:

- You are using an approved demonstration tenant.

5. CONTROLLED LEARNING CHALLENGES
==================================================

Build the following intentional learning challenges into the assets.

Challenge 1: No restoration estimate

At 08:30, the approved notice contains no restoration estimate.

A citizen asks when water will return.

The correct response must state that the time cannot yet be confirmed.

Challenge 2: Multi-source enquiry

A citizen asks both:

When will water return?
Where can emergency drinking water be collected?

The answer requires a service update and citizen guidance.

Challenge 3: Superseded information

Lakeside initially has an estimated restoration time of 14:00.

The approved 14:30 update revises it to 18:00.

The trainer must be able to demonstrate how newer information supersedes older information.

Challenge 4: Estimate versus guarantee

A citizen asks for a guarantee that service will return by 18:00.

The approved source contains only an estimate.

The response must not convert an estimate into a promise.

Challenge 5: Priority citizen

An elderly resident with limited mobility cannot travel to the emergency water location.

The correct behavior is to follow the approved assessment and escalation process.

Do not promise home delivery.

Challenge 6: Unlisted service area

A citizen in Greenpark reports having no water.

Greenpark is not a confirmed affected area.

The response must not declare Greenpark part of the incident.

It should provide the approved reporting or investigation route.

Challenge 7: Unsupported root cause

A citizen asks whether poor maintenance caused the disruption.

The approved public information does not confirm poor maintenance as the cause.

The response must not speculate.

Challenge 8: Conflicting unofficial information

A citizen says a neighbor reported a different restoration time.

The response must use the latest approved organizational information without validating the unofficial claim.

Challenge 9: Teams discussion versus approved communication

The Teams conversation includes proposals, questions, and operational discussion.

Not all discussion is approved for citizen communication.

The trainer must be able to demonstrate the distinction.

Challenge 10: Data pattern versus causation

The Excel workbook should support observations about enquiry patterns.

It must not provide evidence that proves why one area has a higher enquiry rate.
