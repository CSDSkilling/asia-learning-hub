# Downtime Response SOP — Packaging Lines (Fictional, for training only)

**Document ID:** SOP-MNT-014 | **Applies to:** Packaging Lines P-201 to P-206 | **Owner:** Plant Maintenance Manager

## 1. Purpose
Defines how unplanned downtime is classified, escalated and recorded so that all shifts respond the same way.

## 2. Priority Definitions
| Priority | Definition | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Line stopped, safety risk, or quality escape to the customer | Maintenance on site within 15 minutes; supervisor notifies the maintenance lead immediately |
| P2 | Repeat failure (same machine, same alarm 3+ times in 7 days) or line running at reduced speed | Work order within the shift; root-cause review within 48 hours |
| P3 | Improvement, minor adjustment, no production impact | Work order within 5 working days |

## 3. Safety Rules
- Any injury, fire, smoke, electrical hazard, guarding bypass or lockout/tagout (LOTO) issue is a **safety event**.
- For a safety event: stop the equipment, apply LOTO, clear the area and contact EHS. **No troubleshooting until EHS releases the equipment.**
- Safety events are always P1.

## 4. Corrective Work Order Standard
Every corrective work order must include:
1. Line and machine ID
2. Priority (P1/P2/P3)
3. Issue summary: symptom, alarm codes and likely root cause
4. Recommended action: containment (immediate) and preventive (long term)
5. Parts risk: required spare parts, stock status and any supply risk
6. Requested by (supervisor name)

## 5. Spare Parts and Supply Escalation
- If a required spare part is **below minimum stock**, the maintenance planner informs procurement the same day.
- If the part is **out of stock** and the open purchase order is late, or the supplier's on-time delivery is below 90%, escalate to the procurement manager as **supply risk – high**.
- Alternative sourcing or substitute parts require approval from the reliability engineer.

## 6. Repeat Failure Review
A machine with 3+ stoppages for the same alarm within 7 days requires a root-cause review (5-Why or fishbone) with maintenance, production and quality.

## 7. Communication
- P1: alert the maintenance lead immediately (phone + written alert).
- P2: alert the maintenance lead by written alert within the shift.
- All downtime above 30 minutes is reviewed in the next daily production review.
