# Past Investigation: Connector Alignment

Synthetic Contoso training record. Fictional incident year: 2024. Location: Plant B, Line 2, Sensor Pack.

## Summary

A connector assembly fixture was incorrectly adjusted after maintenance. The first-inspection pass rate fell because connectors did not seat correctly. This closed historical case concerns a different line and a mechanical failure mechanism.

## Symptom

Inspection found connectors positioned outside the assembly drawing's tolerance. Soldered connections passed their checks; the repeated failure was connector seating.

## Investigation

- Engineers measured connector position and compared it with the approved assembly drawing.
- Failures followed use of one fixture, not a change in oven temperature, soldering material batch, or operator.
- Inspection found an incorrectly adjusted locating stop following fixture maintenance.
- Correcting the fixture position restored acceptable seating with the same operators and materials.

## Root cause

The fixture's locating stop was incorrectly adjusted, and the post-maintenance first-piece check did not detect the error. The confirmed cause was mechanical positioning, not oven heating conditions.

## Corrective action

Engineering restored the approved fixture position, verified the first assembled units, and added a documented fixture-position check after maintenance.

## Lessons

Match a precedent by product, line, defect type, and mechanism, not merely by a lower pass rate. This Sensor Pack fixture finding does not support a Power Module oven hypothesis. Do not transfer a mechanical remedy to another incident without matching physical evidence and engineering review.
