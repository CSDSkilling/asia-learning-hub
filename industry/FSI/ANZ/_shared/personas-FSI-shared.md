# FSI personas

Before describing a feature, identify who performs the process, which decisions they make, what
evidence they need, what can go wrong, and which measure they own.

---

## Banking

| Persona | Goals and decisions | Pressures | Opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| Branch Manager | Customer experience, service quality, staff performance, growth, escalation | Service volumes, capacity, complaints, revenue expectations | Where does the team lose the most time serving or following up? |
| Relationship Manager | Client understanding, opportunities, preparation, follow-up, portfolio growth | Information scattered across email, CRM, documents and experts | What preparation is manual before a client conversation? |
| Loan Officer | Completeness, policy checks, credit coordination, approval progress, communication | Missing documents, handoffs, backlogs, status enquiries | Which step delays a clear decision or customer update? |
| Risk Officer | Risk identification, monitoring, limits, escalation, evidence | Data quality, model oversight, emerging risk, fragmented reporting | Which decisions need better evidence, faster analysis, or clearer lineage? |
| Compliance Officer | Policy interpretation, monitoring, evidence, reporting, remediation | Changing obligations, manual evidence gathering, audit pressure | Where is it hard to find policy or prove a control operated? |

## Insurance

| Persona | Goals and decisions | Pressures | Opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claims Adjuster | Coverage, evidence, damage, reserve or settlement recommendation, communication | Large files, missing evidence, fraud signals, urgency | What must be assembled before a claim can move confidently? |
| Underwriter | Risk selection, pricing, terms, referrals, exceptions, portfolio quality | Incomplete submissions, inconsistent evidence, explainability, turnaround | Which evidence is hardest to obtain or compare? |

## Wealth & Superannuation

| Persona | Goals and decisions | Pressures | Opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wealth Advisor | Client goals, suitability, portfolio conversations, market context | Research volume, preparation time, documentation, personalization | What would shift time from assembling context to serving clients? |
| Member Services Lead | Member enquiries, switching, retention, contribution and balance questions | Volume, product complexity, consistency of explanation | Which member questions get inconsistent answers across channels? |

*Note: the Member Services persona is an extension for the superannuation sector rather than a
standard financial services persona. Validate the framing against the organisation's own role
definitions before you rely on it in a delivery.*

## Executive and technology stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Focus | Trainer emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Business executive | Growth, service, efficiency, risk, capacity, measurable change | Lead with process and outcome; keep technology proportionate |
| Operations leader | Throughput, backlog, exceptions, handoffs, quality, service levels | Show workflow change, human review, measurable results |
| CIO / CTO | Architecture, integration, platform strategy, scale, security, cost | Clarify systems of record, data flows, identity, extensibility, boundaries |
| CISO / risk / legal / compliance leader | Protection, access, auditability, policy, explainability, accountability | Demonstrate control thinking early — not as a final slide |
