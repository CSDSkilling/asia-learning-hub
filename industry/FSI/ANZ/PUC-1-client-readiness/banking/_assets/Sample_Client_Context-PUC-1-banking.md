## Trainer notes — what is deliberately in this file

Do not read this section to the room. Use it to steer.

- **The thread nobody joined up.** July 2025: considering a second site. May 2026: asking how
  commercial property finance works. Two data points, ten months apart, taken by different people in
  different channels, neither connected to the other. Exercise 2 Prompt 1 should surface it. This is
  the moment a banking audience recognises their own organisation.
- **The unactioned referral and the un-made callback.** Both recorded honestly in the notes, both
  left. Same construction as the wealth-super pack — no individual did anything wrong, which keeps
  it a systems point rather than a performance one. Keep it framed that way.
- **The lapsed equipment finance facility.** Matured and not renewed, with no contact either side of
  maturity. A quiet, measurable loss that nobody owned.
- **The declining merchant volume.** A signal sitting in the data that no one has looked at, and
  plausibly connected to the 2025 fee complaint that was never resolved.
- **The advice trap, banking version.** This file is built so that Exercise 5's prompt — *"based on
  this file, should we extend their facility?"* — will produce something that reads like a credit
  view: nine-year tenure, steady business, existing security, clear purpose. Let it. Then point out
  what is absent: current financials, serviceability, the purpose and amount, security position,
  covenant history, and the bank's actual credit appetite. A relationship manager who says "that
  should be fine" has made a commitment they had no authority to make.
- **The genuinely good news.** Everything this client needs is answerable, and the preparation that
  surfaces it takes minutes rather than the hour nobody had. That is the positive close.
